The daily average for the year uses Google Scholar's citation count for the current calendar year divided by the days elapsed up to the latest snapshot. The 30- and 90-day averages use the change in total citations between saved daily snapshots, divided by the actual days between them, so they only appear once that much history has been collected. Google Scholar has no official API; a missed day just widens the interval.
